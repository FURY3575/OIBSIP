From tubing to trailhead

A frame takes us three weeks, start to finish. No batching, no shortcuts — one builder sees your frame through every stage.

01

Fit consultation

We measure you, talk through your riding, and mock up geometry before ordering a single tube.

02

Hand-mitred & brazed

Tubes are mitred, tacked, and fillet-brazed in-house, then filed smooth by hand.

03

Paint & delivery

Frames are finished in our two-stage paint booth and shipped or handed over in person.

The Continental 631

Our do-it-all touring frame — loaded for a week in the backcountry or stripped down for a fast Sunday loop.

Built from Reynolds 631 air-hardened steel, the Continental holds its line loaded with panniers and still feels lively unloaded on a club ride.

Every frame ships with a lifetime crack warranty and a fit card documenting your exact geometry, so a repaint or a repair never means starting over.
